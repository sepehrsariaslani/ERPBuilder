# Final polish checklist

A page is not finished when it is merely correct. After business correctness, permissions, accessibility, workflow, and canonical component reuse pass, perform this craft pass.

## 1. Visual direction
- Is one visual thesis stated?
- Does the direction fit the task: calm, dense, operational, premium, or transactional?
- Is there only one dominant visual idea?

## 2. Composition
- Does the 3-second scan reveal identity, status, exception, and next action?
- Are major edges aligned?
- Is grouping achieved mostly through spacing/alignment rather than cards?
- Is the primary work more prominent than supporting sidebars?
- Does density match the task?

## 3. Typography
- Are semantic type roles consistent?
- Are Persian labels readable at real content lengths?
- Are numbers, currency, quantity, and time aligned cleanly?
- Is secondary text still readable?
- Are IDs/technical values visually secondary unless needed?

## 4. Spacing and rhythm
- Does the page use existing spacing tokens?
- Are repeated patterns spaced consistently?
- Are related controls closer to each other than unrelated controls?
- Is there enough breathing room without wasting operational space?

## 5. Color and emphasis
- Is semantic color used only for meaning?
- Does one accent dominate rather than several?
- Is the primary action obvious without making everything colorful?
- Does the page work in light and dark themes?

## 6. Components
- Were canonical components reused?
- Are there local one-off cards, buttons, dropdowns, tables, or status chips that should be removed?
- Does every new component have a proven role?

## 7. Signature
- Is there one useful memorable element on an important page?
- Does it communicate real information?
- Are surrounding elements quiet enough to let it work?

## 8. Micro-interactions
- Are hover/focus/pressed/busy/success/error states polished?
- Do async states avoid layout jumps?
- Is save/submit feedback immediate and honest?
- Is direct manipulation clear before and after commit?
- Does reduced motion preserve all meaning?

## 9. Content
- Do labels describe outcomes precisely?
- Are empty/error states actionable?
- Is technical/backend vocabulary hidden from users?
- Does Persian copy sound concise and professional?

## 10. Responsive
- Is mobile intentionally recomposed rather than shrunk?
- Do long Persian labels, keyboard, tables, side panels, and fixed bars remain usable?
- Is the same business capability reachable?

## 11. Restraint
Attempt to remove:
- one unnecessary card;
- one unnecessary border;
- one unnecessary icon;
- one unnecessary accent color;
- one unnecessary line of copy;
- one unnecessary animation.

Keep the removal only if clarity and capability stay equal or improve.

## 12. Final question

Can a user understand what is happening, know what to do next, complete the task quickly, and feel that every visible detail was intentional?

If yes, the page is both operationally correct and visually finished.
