# Gantt

Show scale, today, task identity, duration, dependencies, and exceptions clearly. Support zoom/scroll without losing row identity. Dragging or resizing a task must preview date and dependency effects, validate before save, and provide undo/cancel plus a keyboard or form path.

Keep precise dates and status available outside color and geometry. On a small screen, prioritize schedule decisions in a list/timeline view; do not merely shrink the desktop chart.
