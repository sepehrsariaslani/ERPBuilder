# Kanban

Use columns for meaningful business states, not arbitrary decoration. Show counts, owner, due/blocked state, and the permitted transition. If cards can be dragged, provide clear drop targets, validation before committing, immediate feedback, and a keyboard/action-menu equivalent. Preserve the native workflow and permission rules.

Do not assume every state can transition to every other state. When a move is blocked, give the reason and the next action. Keep card contents limited to what supports triage; open full details predictably.
