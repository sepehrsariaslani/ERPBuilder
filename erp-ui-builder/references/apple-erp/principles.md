# Principles for Hesabyar

Apply these as decisions, not a visual style. Purpose: one clear job for each page and element. Agency: visible control, cancellation, safe back navigation, and recovery. Responsibility: honest state, permissions, provenance, and consequences for financial or sensitive actions. Familiarity: preserve established Hesabyar and web ERP behavior. Flexibility: work across viewport, input, ability, and text size. Simplicity: show exactly what this decision needs while keeping domain complexity accessible. Craft: finish alignment, spacing, type, copy, focus, loading, responsive and performance behavior. Delight: make completion and recovery feel fluent; avoid decorative motion.

The design test is: can a new user tell where they are, what happened, what matters, and what to do next without learning backend structure? Give each page one main task and one primary action per context. These are separate from lifecycle controls that must remain visible for accounting correctness. Prefer real business language and native ERPNext/Frappe status and transitions. Never hide audit data or controls merely to make a page look minimal.

## Master synthesis

For significant new pages, redesigns, and shared components, also read [design masters](design-masters.md). Use the masters as complementary lenses:

- **Jobs:** define the essence and remove competing product goals.
- **Ive:** achieve simplicity by understanding and absorbing complexity, not by hiding it.
- **Atkinson + Tesler:** make common interaction direct, visible, consistent, and testable.
- **Kare:** make symbols legible, memorable, and restrained.
- **Raskin:** protect attention, reduce hidden modes, and design around cognition rather than implementation.
- **Norman:** match the user's conceptual model, expose signifiers, give feedback, and design recovery.
- **Tognazzini:** preserve work, stable targets, discoverability, and operator efficiency.
- **Rams:** remove decoration and exaggeration; keep what is useful, understandable, honest, and durable.
- **Esslinger:** maintain one coherent product language across modules while allowing restrained identity.
- **Tufte:** make analytical evidence comparable, exact, and visually honest.
- **Nielsen/Molich:** use established usability heuristics as a final gap check.

Do not invoke a designer's name as the reason for a decision. Convert the lens into a local product rule and user effect. If the lenses disagree, resolve the conflict in this order: business/data correctness and permissions → accessibility and recoverability → established Hesabyar patterns → task efficiency → visual craft.

Source inspiration: [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/), [Apple Design Skill](https://github.com/dickwu/apple-design-skill), and the provenance links in [design masters](design-masters.md). Apply web ERP conventions where platform-specific HIG guidance conflicts with the product.
